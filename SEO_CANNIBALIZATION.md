# SEO Cannibalization Audit — P2.2

Scope: 108 indexable localized pages (36 RU + 36 GE + 36 EN).

## Method

Checked page-level search intent using the P2.1 keyword map and compared current titles/H1s across language roots, provider pages, Tbilisi service pages, Tbilisi district handyman pages, Batumi service pages and Batumi district handyman pages.

RU / GE / EN versions are treated as language variants, not as cannibalizing pages.

## Confirmed overlap

### Tbilisi root vs Tbilisi handyman-by-hour page

The following root pages used the same or nearly the same primary wording as the dedicated Tbilisi handyman-by-hour page:

| Language | Root | Dedicated page | Conflict |
|---|---|---|---|
| RU | /ru/ — title targeted “Мастер на час Тбилиси” | /ru/tbilisi/master-na-chas-tbilisi/ — “Мастер на час в Тбилиси” | Confirmed |
| GE | /ge/ — title targeted “ოსტატი საათით თბილისში” | /ge/tbilisi/master-na-chas-tbilisi/ — “ოსტატი საათით თბილისში” | Confirmed |
| EN | /en/ — “Handyman Tbilisi” | /en/tbilisi/master-na-chas-tbilisi/ — “Handyman in Tbilisi” | Material overlap; root is broader but too close to the dedicated page |

### Resolution

The root pages are the general city-level matching/service-entry pages. The dedicated pages retain the narrower handyman-by-hour / small-home-tasks intent.

Root titles were widened to describe the general matching service, while the dedicated pages remain unchanged.

No URL consolidation, redirects, canonical changes, or removal of district/service pages is required.

## Non-conflicts

- Tbilisi service pages are separated by service + city.
- Batumi service pages are separated by service + city.
- Tbilisi district pages are separated by district and target local handyman intent.
- Batumi district pages are likewise separated by district.
- The provider page is a separate provider/B2B intent and is not a consumer-service duplicate.
- RU, GE and EN pages are localized equivalents and are not treated as cannibalization.
