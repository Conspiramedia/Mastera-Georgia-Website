What we do with doors and locks

  • Interior door fitting — frame assembly, hanging the leaf, mortising the lock and handle, architraves and extension jambs
  • Entrance door fitting — anchoring into the opening, foam sealing, jambs and threshold, adjusting the seal pressure
  • Lock and cylinder replacement — mortise and rim locks, new cylinder when keys are lost or still held by previous tenants
  • Adjusting sagging doors — tightening hinges, fixing catching and gaps that stop the door closing
  • Hinge replacement — new hinges in place of worn ones, moving hinges to the other side when rehanging
  • Door closers — fitting a closer on an entrance, office or flat door with closing speed adjustment
  • Hardware mortising — handles, latches, viewers, chains, stops — clean mortising without chipping the leaf
  • Minor door repairs — filling chips, replacing seals, repairing handles and latches, stopping squeaks

Door and lock installation in Batumi: what to keep in mind

In Batumi lock demand is tied closely to the rental market. Flats in the high-rises by the New Boulevard and in Old Batumi are let daily, tenants change year round, and owners replace cylinders regularly — after a long let, when handing the flat to a management company, or simply between seasons.

The coastal climate adds its own problem: humidity and salt in the air accelerate corrosion of the mechanism, and locks on entrance doors, especially in buildings near the waterfront, seize up noticeably earlier than usual. There is one warning sign — the key starts turning stiffly. Better to replace the cylinder in advance than to force the door once the key snaps inside it.

How much does changing a lock cost in Batumi?

Cylinder replacement — from 40₾, mortise lock replacement — from 50₾. Interior door fitted — from 130₾, entrance door — from 200₾. Finding a master is free.

I have just moved in — should I change the lock?

Replacing the cylinder is enough — cheaper than a whole lock and it solves the real problem: keys held by previous tenants and agents stop working. It takes about half an hour, from 40₾.

The door catches on the frame and will not close — is that a repair?

Usually not. Most often the door has dropped on its hinges and adjustment fixes it — from 40₾. If the hinges are worn they get replaced, from 50₾. The leaf itself does not need changing.

Do I buy the door in advance?

Yes, you buy the door. The master measures and fits it. Before buying it is worth having a master measure up — openings are rarely square, and the leaf and frame are sized to fit.

The door slammed shut with the keys inside — can you help?

Yes, as an urgent call-out. Tick "Urgent" in the request and it goes out flagged. The master opens the lock and can fit a new cylinder on the spot, from 40₾.
