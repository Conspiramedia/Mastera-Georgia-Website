Cleaning Service in Batumi: Apartments and Homes

Cleaning services in Batumi are available for apartments and homes in Old Batumi, New Boulevard, the Khimshiashvili area and Boni. You can request one-time or regular cleaning, including cleaning before or after a move and after renovation work.

In your request, include the property type, approximate size and the kind of cleaning needed. For post-renovation cleaning, many windows or deep cleaning of the kitchen and bathrooms, describing the scope in advance helps plan the service.
