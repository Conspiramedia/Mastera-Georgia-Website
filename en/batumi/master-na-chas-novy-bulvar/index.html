Handyman in the New Boulevard district

New Boulevard is the modern stretch of the coast: the Alphabet Tower, the Dolphinarium, Lake Ardagani and the line of towers along the seafront. The area is almost entirely new builds and apartments let by the night or by the month. The most common jobs here come right after handover: assembling furniture, mounting appliances, fitting curtain rails and small repairs before tenants move in.

Masters who already work in New Boulevard respond the fastest — no need to wait for someone to travel from the other side of the city. Describe your task, and the nearest available master will contact you within 60 minutes.

How quickly will a handyman arrive in New Boulevard?

There are plenty of available masters nearby in New Boulevard — along Chavchavadze and Abashidze Avenue, by New Boulevard Park and Bagebi. First response on average in 15–60 minutes, with a same-day visit possible.

How much does a handyman cost in New Boulevard?

Prices in New Boulevard are the same as across the city: an hour of work — from 40₾, a one-off small task — from 20₾, minimum call-out — from 30₾. Finding a master through the service is free.

Will you hang a TV and pictures in a rented flat without damage?

Yes. New Boulevard has a lot of business-class rental housing and new developments — masters carefully mount TVs, shelves, mirrors and pictures, taking the expensive finishes into account. For complex mounting there's a dedicated mounting service.

What's included in the handyman service?

Any small one-off tasks: hang, screw in, tighten, secure, adjust, help around the home — several jobs in one visit. For bigger jobs we have dedicated masters: furniture assembly, mounting & installation, home repairs.
