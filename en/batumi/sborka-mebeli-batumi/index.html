What furniture we assemble

  • Cabinet furniture — wardrobes, sliding wardrobes, chests, cabinets, shelving. Assembly with or without instructions, facade fitting, door and hinge adjustment
  • Kitchen units — fitting lower and upper modules, wall-mounting cabinets, countertop installation, sink and hob cut-outs
  • Bedroom furniture — beds, beds with lifting mechanisms, bases, bedside tables. Reinforcing joints, eliminating squeaks
  • IKEA and other-store furniture — assembled by instructions and part numbers, with missing fasteners brought along and completeness checked
  • Disassembly and assembly for relocation — careful disassembly, labelling of parts and fasteners, reassembly at the new place with nothing lost
  • Office furniture — desks, shelving, document cabinets, conference furniture, chairs
  • Furniture repair and restoration — replacing hinges, runners, handles, legs. Eliminating play, misalignment and squeaks
  • Mounting and installation — shelves, mirrors, wall-mounted cabinets with secure fixing suited to the wall type

Furniture Assembly in Batumi: Homes and Moves

A furniture assembler in Batumi can visit Old Batumi, New Boulevard, the Khimshiashvili area and Boni. Common jobs include assembling wardrobes, beds, kitchen units, tables and chests of drawers after delivery or a move.

In your request, include the furniture brand or model, the number of items and whether old furniture also needs to be disassembled. For large furniture, it is useful to mention the floor and whether an elevator is available, especially for wardrobes and kitchen units.

How to call a furniture assembler in Batumi?

How much does it cost to assemble a wardrobe?

Prices depend on the item: bed assembly — from 40₾, wardrobe — from 60₾, kitchen unit — from 50₾ per linear metre. Finding a master is free. The master quotes the exact price before starting.

How quickly will an assembler arrive?

First response — on average 15–60 minutes. You can also book a convenient time, e.g. to match your furniture delivery.

Do you assemble IKEA furniture?

Yes. Masters assemble IKEA furniture and items from any other store using the instructions and part numbers — wardrobes, kitchens, beds, shelving and chests.

What if fasteners or parts are missing?
  1. Masters carry a stock of fasteners — confirmats, euro screws, dowels, brackets
  2. Missing small parts are brought along or sourced on site
  3. Completeness is checked before assembly begins
Do you guarantee the assembly?

Yes. All assemblers are verified; before choosing you can see ratings and reviews. The master eliminates play and misalignment, checks all mechanisms and guarantees the assembly.
