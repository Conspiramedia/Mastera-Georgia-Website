Washing machine connection in Batumi: what to keep in mind

In Batumi many flats are let daily and monthly, so a washing machine here is often connected in rental housing — at check-in, after a change of tenants, or when the owner swaps appliances between seasons. Such requests are usually urgent: the machine arrived today and the flat is let tomorrow.

High humidity near the sea adds two local details. The machine should not sit flush against the wall in a cramped unventilated bathroom — the back panel stays damp and the drum starts to smell. And the socket next to the machine must be splash-proof: in Batumi that is not a formality but something masters run into on call-outs constantly.
