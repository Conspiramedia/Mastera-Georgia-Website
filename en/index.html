FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a call-out cost?

The call-out is included in the price of the work. The specialist quotes the cost after clarifying the details or looking at the job — before starting. If the price does not suit you, you pay nothing.

How soon will someone arrive?

A reply usually arrives within the hour and a visit is possible the same day. Mark urgent requests and they go out to specialists first.

Do you vet your specialists?

Yes. Every specialist registers with documents, and clients rate the work afterwards. Those with low ratings stop receiving requests.

If the work is done badly?

Contact support and we will look into it. Where applicable the specialist gives a guarantee, and disputes are settled from messages and photos.

What languages can I use?

Russian, English and Georgian. We handle requests in any of them and find a specialist who speaks your language.
