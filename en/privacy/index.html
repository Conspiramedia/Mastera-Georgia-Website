Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Mastera service collects through mastera.ge and its messaging bots, why we need it, who receives it and how to have it deleted. Processing follows the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, as in force since 1 March 2024.

1. Who processes the data

The Mastera service, Tbilisi, Georgia, is the controller — it decides what data is collected and why. For any question about your data, contact support on Telegram @masterasupport or by phone +995 557 645 196.

2. What data we collect

From a client request: name, a contact for getting in touch (phone, Telegram or WhatsApp), city and district, a description of the job and, if you attached them, photos. The form never asks for your exact address: the specialist learns it from you on the call.

From specialists: name, contact, trade, service area, plus a photo of an ID document and a selfie — these confirm the identity of the person who will come to your home. These documents are never shown to clients.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed.

We do not collect special categories of data — health, religion, political views — and never ask about them in the form.

3. Why we process it

Request data serves exactly one purpose: passing your job to specialists and letting one of them reach you. Specialists' data is used to verify them and to settle payments. Technical data is for visit statistics and protecting the site from spam.

The legal basis is your consent, given when you submit the form, and the need to provide the service you asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Who receives the data

The job description, city and district are visible to specialists working in that district. Your contact is visible only to the specialist who takes the job — until then it stays hidden.

We do not sell data and do not pass it to third parties for marketing. Data may be disclosed to public authorities only on a lawful request.

5. Where the data is stored

The service runs on servers outside Georgia: requests are handled by a Telegram bot hosted with a hosting provider. Data travels between your device, the site and the bot over an encrypted connection.

6. Retention and security

Contact details are stored encrypted. Only the service administrator and the specialist who took your job can see them. Data is kept for as long as the work and any dispute about it may require.

7. Your rights

You have the right to learn whether we process your data and to get a copy; to correct inaccuracies; to request deletion; to withdraw consent; and to object to processing. Send your request to support at @masterasupport — we reply within the period set by law.

If you believe your rights have been breached, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia (personaldata.ge).

8. Cookies

The site uses cookies and browser local storage to remember your chosen language and city. You can delete or block them in your browser settings — the site will keep working.

9. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. The date of the last change is shown below; we will announce significant changes on the site.
