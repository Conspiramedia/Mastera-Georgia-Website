Air conditioner installation in Tbilisi: what to keep in mind

In Tbilisi an air conditioner runs for cooling from May to October, with the peak load in July and August when the city holds around 35 °C. Installation requests in spring far outnumber available masters, so fitting a split system is cheaper and faster in March and April, before the season starts.

Soviet-era blocks in Gldani, Varketili and Temka mean concrete walls and outdoor units at a height that needs a rope access technician or a lift platform. In the old housing of Sololaki and Mtatsminda the walls are thick brick and drilling the line takes longer. Masters account for this when quoting, so it is worth stating the floor and building type in your request.

How much does air conditioner installation cost in Tbilisi?

Split system up to 12 BTU — from 150₾, 18–24 BTU — from 200₾. Cleaning — from 60₾, refrigerant recharge — from 80₾. Finding a master is free; the master quotes before starting.

Do I buy the air conditioner myself?

Yes, you buy the unit. The master does the installation and can advise on capacity: a 20 m² room usually needs 9 BTU, a 30 m² room 12 BTU. Line consumables the master brings along.

How often should an air conditioner be cleaned?

Once a year, ideally in spring before the season. A clogged filter and heat exchanger cut cooling and raise the electricity bill, and mould grows in the drain — that is the smell when you switch on.

The air conditioner is leaking indoors — is that expensive?

Usually not. In most cases the drain is blocked, and cleaning solves it from 60₾. Less often it is the line fall or a cracked tray — then the master quotes after inspection.

Can the air conditioner be moved when I relocate?

Yes. Removal with refrigerant recovery — from 80₾, relocating the unit — from 120₾. If the line does not reach, the master runs an extra one — from 25₾ per metre.
