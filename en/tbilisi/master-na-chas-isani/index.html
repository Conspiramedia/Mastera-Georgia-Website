Handyman in the Isani district

Isani is a large residential district on the left bank of the Kura: the Isani metro, Ketevan Tsamebuli Avenue (Queen Ketevan), dense multi-storey housing from the Soviet period. These are residential blocks with a great many typical flats, so the most frequent tasks in Isani are small home repairs and maintenance: replacing a lock, adjusting doors and windows, hanging shelves, assembling furniture.

Local masters work in the district who know the typical layouts and arrive quickly from Isani and neighbouring Samgori. Describe your task — the nearest master will contact you within 60 minutes.

What a handyman does in Isani

  • Small home repairs — replace a lock, tighten hinges, adjust doors and windows in typical flats — a frequent request in Isani
  • Small plumbing and electrical jobs — replace a faucet, fit a socket, fix a minor fault (for bigger work — dedicated masters)
  • Mounting and assembly — hang shelves and curtain rails, assemble furniture in a flat
  • Help around the home — "handyman by the hour": one-off errands where no specialist is needed

Call a handyman in Isani?

How quickly will a handyman arrive in Isani?

Isani is a large residential district by the Isani metro with its own local masters. First response on average in 15–60 minutes, with a same-day visit possible.

Do you do small repairs in the typical buildings of Isani?

Yes. Isani is dominated by Soviet-era housing, so frequent tasks are lock replacement, door and window adjustment, small plumbing and wiring repairs, and mounting. For bigger jobs there's a plumber and an electrician.

How much does a handyman cost in Isani?

Prices are the same across the city: an hour of work — from 40₾, a one-off small task — from 20₾, minimum call-out — from 90₾. Finding a master through the service is free.

What's included in the handyman service?

Any small one-off tasks: hang, screw in, tighten, secure, adjust, help around the home — several jobs in one visit. For bigger jobs we have dedicated masters: furniture assembly, mounting & installation, home repairs.

Are the masters verified?

Yes. All masters are verified. You see rating and client reviews before choosing.
