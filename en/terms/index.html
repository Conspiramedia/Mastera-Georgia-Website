Terms of Service

These terms describe how the Mastera service works: what it does, what it does not do, who is responsible for the work and how disputes are resolved. By submitting a request through mastera.ge or our bots, you accept these terms.

1. What Mastera is

Mastera is a service that matches you with specialists for household work in Tbilisi and Batumi. We take your request, pass it to suitable specialists and connect you with one who is ready to take the job.

We do not carry out the work ourselves. Repairs, installation and any other work are done by the specialist, an independent contractor. The service agreement is between you and the specialist.

2. How it works

You describe the job in the form on the site or in the bot. The request goes to specialists working in your district in the relevant trade. The specialist who takes it receives your contact and gets in touch, usually within an hour during working hours.

The specialist clarifies the job, visits to inspect it if needed and quotes a price. You learn the price before any work starts and decide whether to go ahead. You may decline at any point before the work begins.

3. Prices and payment

Prices on the site are indicative: they show the general range, not an exact figure. The final price is quoted by the specialist once they understand the job.

You pay the specialist directly, after the work is done, in the way you agree with them. The service does not take payment for the work and is not an intermediary in settlements.

4. Who is responsible for what

The specialist who did the work is responsible for its quality, timing and keeping to what was agreed. Complaints about the work are addressed to them.

The service is responsible for its own part: verifying specialists' identity at registration, passing on your request and protecting your data. We do not guarantee the outcome of the work and do not compensate damage caused by a specialist — but we do review complaints and suspend specialists who attract them repeatedly.

5. If something goes wrong

Write to support at @masterasupport and describe what happened. We will contact the specialist, help sort it out and, if the complaint is confirmed, take action — up to suspending the specialist from receiving requests.

Complaints affect a specialist's rating: if it falls below the threshold, they stop receiving requests.

6. What we ask of you

Describe the job honestly and give a working contact — otherwise the specialist cannot reach you and the request takes someone else's place. Do not send requests that contain no real job.

Make it possible for the specialist to work: access to the site and safe conditions.

7. Specialist verification

Specialists pass an identity check at registration: we ask for an ID document and a selfie so we know who comes to a client's home. This is not a check of qualifications and not a warranty of work quality.

8. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. The date of the last change is shown below. For requests already submitted, the version in force at the time of submission applies.

9. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the law of Georgia. The parties seek to settle disputes through support; if no agreement is reached, in the manner established by Georgian law.

How we handle personal data is described in the privacy policy.
